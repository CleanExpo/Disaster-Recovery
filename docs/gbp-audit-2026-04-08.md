## 2. Critical Findings

### 2.1 No GBP Knowledge Panel in Google Search (CRITICAL)

Searching for the brand name + domain in Google Search returns **no knowledge panel** on the right side. This means either:
- The GBP is **not verified** — Google hasn't accepted ownership confirmation
- The GBP exists but has so little data/activity that Google doesn't trigger it
- The business name doesn't match what Google has indexed

**Action required:** Login to Google Business Profile Manager (business.google.com) and confirm the profile shows as "Verified". If not, complete verification.

### 2.2 Zero Google Reviews (CRITICAL — GAP-074)

No Google reviews exist. Ask Maps relies heavily on review content to match conversational queries. A business with zero reviews is functionally invisible.

**Target:** 10 reviews by 14 April 2026. Send the review request email (template in Section 6 of strategy doc) to all past clients in the `Job` table with `status = 'completed'` and `customerEmail IS NOT NULL`.

### 2.3 Brisbane-Centric Service Area Framing (CRITICAL — GAP-049)

All publicly observable signals describe a Brisbane/SEQ business:
- **Address:** 17 Tile St Unit 4, Wacol QLD 4076 (inner Brisbane suburb)
- **Business name:** "Disaster Recovery | Wacol QLD"
- **ZoomInfo:** "specializes in emergency property restoration services across South East Queensland"
- **LinkedIn:** "Disaster Recovery QLD" — industry Construction, 2-10 employees, Wacol QLD
- **Google-indexed suburb pages:** Rocklea, Toowong, Strathpine, Ipswich, Aspley — all SEQ

When a homeowner in Cairns asks Ask Maps "who can restore my cyclone-damaged home", this platform will not appear.

**Action required:** Update GBP service area to national (see Section 3.2 of strategy doc for full LGA list).
