## The Vision

**Solve the problem for small and medium business owners in the Professional Cleaning and Restoration industry — and in doing so, deliver the standard of service that every customer deserves.**

Disaster Recovery Australia and the NRPG network exist because two groups of people are being failed by the current model: the business owner who has spent years building expertise and reputation, and the property owner who calls for help at the worst moment of their lives. We fix both sides of that problem at once.

---

## The Problem We Are Solving

The current disaster recovery industry is dominated by a **Builder/Restorer model** that was never designed to serve the customer.

When an insurer or property owner engages a builder to manage restoration work, the commercial interests of that builder sit directly against the interests of the property owner:

- **Higher costs** — builder margin layered over restoration contractor margin
- **Time delays** — coordination overhead, scope decisions made to protect builder margin not to accelerate recovery
- **Rising insurance premiums** — inflated scopes, extended timelines, and disputed claims feed directly into premium increases for everyone

This model benefits one party: the builder. The customer pays for it. The specialist restorer — who holds the certifications, the equipment, and the expertise — becomes a subcontractor to a generalist who controls the scope.

**NRPG exists to eliminate the middleman.**

## Who First — The Brand Behind the Business

The phrase "Who First" did not come from a marketing agency. It came from a close friend outside the industry — someone who genuinely had no idea what disaster recovery was, or who you would call when a flood, fire, or mould emergency hit your property.

That conversation revealed the industry's core problem: **the professional restorer is invisible to the people who need them most.**

When something goes wrong with a property — a burst pipe, a house fire, flood damage, a mould outbreak — the question most people ask is *"Who do I call?"* They call their insurer. They call a plumber. They call a builder. They almost never call a certified restorer directly, because they don't know the restorer exists, or what the restorer does, or why it matters.

**Who First** is the answer to that question. When there is an emergency or an indoor environment issue, the first call should be to a Professional Restorer — not a builder, not an insurer's preferred contractor.

> *Who First = Disaster Recovery | The Professional Restorers*

Marketing and communicating what the restoration industry does — and why it matters — is one of the most important challenges NRPG must overcome. Our brand position is not a slogan. It is the strategic answer to industry invisibility.

## What Makes NRPG Different

Every contractor in the NRPG network is held to a standard that no builder/restorer model can replicate:

**1. The 22-Module Onboarding System**
Every contractor completes a structured 22-module onboarding program before joining the active network. This is not an orientation — it is a comprehensive assessment of technical competency, business practices, customer service standards, and claims documentation capability.

**2. Mandatory IICRC Certification**
All NRPG members hold current IICRC certification relevant to their service category. The IICRC is the international benchmark for the restoration industry. There are no exceptions to this requirement.

**3. Minimum 2 Years Industry Experience**
Certification alone is not sufficient. Every NRPG contractor has a minimum of two years' practical experience in the restoration industry before admission to the network.

**4. Industry Association Membership**
All contractors must be active members of a relevant industry association. Professional belonging signals commitment to the industry beyond individual commercial interests.

**5. CARSI Membership — Mandatory Continuing Education**
All NRPG members are members of CARSI and complete ongoing IICRC-approved continuing education through CARSI's online CEC course platform. Certification is not a one-time event — it is maintained through ongoing learning.

**6. RestoreAssist — The Field Operations Platform**
Every job is managed and documented through the RestoreAssist app. This provides real-time job documentation, moisture reading records, drying logs, photographic evidence, and claim-ready reports. The documentation standard that insurers and AFCA require is built into every job from day one.

**7. Not a Builder/Restorer**
NRPG members are Professional Restorers. The network does not include builders operating as restorers. This is the clearest structural distinction between NRPG and the model we are displacing.

## Where We Are Going

**Growth — always.**
The NRPG network grows by bringing certified, experienced, independent restorers into a system that amplifies their capability without removing their independence. Growth is not measured in headcount alone — it is measured in the quality of outcomes delivered and the number of customers who received the standard of service they were entitled to.

**Recognition — for the industry.**
The Professional Cleaning and Restoration industry deserves to be known. Every Australian should understand the difference between a builder and a certified restorer, what IICRC certification means, and why the first call after a property disaster should be to a professional restorer. NRPG carries the responsibility for making that recognition happen — through the quality of our work, the consistency of our documentation, and the strength of the "Who First" brand.

**Independence — back to the business owner.**
The business owners in the NRPG network built their expertise over years. Many of them built their businesses independently, without the support structure that larger operators have. NRPG returns power and profile to those business owners — not by absorbing them into a corporate model, but by giving them the platform, the certification framework, the technology, and the brand recognition to grow on their own terms.

The measure of our success is not NRPG's revenue. It is the independence, growth, and recognition of the businesses in our network — and the quality of service received by every customer those businesses serve.
