# Olivia FAQ — Onboarding Questions

_Last updated: 2026-04-26._

Frequently asked questions from prospective contractors. The answers
below are factual and PUBLIC class. Olivia uses these to ground her
responses; for anything not here she should refuse and offer to
escalate to a human onboarding coordinator.

## Cost and fees

**How much does it cost to join?**
Specific subscription rates and platform fees are confirmed in
writing during onboarding — Olivia cannot quote a figure on a call.
The model is a recurring subscription for network access plus a
per-job platform fee. NRPG does not take a share of the contractor's
restoration revenue.

**Is there a joining fee or one-off setup fee?**
Confirmed in writing during onboarding. Same answer category as above.

**Does NRPG take a percentage of my restoration revenue?**
No. The contractor bills the client directly for restoration work
and keeps that revenue. NRPG charges a subscription plus per-job
platform fee — separate from the contractor's revenue.

## Insurance and certification

**What insurance do I need?**
Public liability is required for every category. Workers compensation
where applicable to your state or territory. Professional indemnity is
recommended and required for some categories. Specific cover thresholds
are listed on the application surface.

**My IICRC certification is in the US — does that count?**
The IICRC register is global. As long as the registration is current
and verifiable on the public register, it counts. NRPG verifies
against the live register at application time and at renewal.

**Can I apply if I'm not yet IICRC-certified?**
Not at this time — IICRC certification is a hard requirement. Apply
once you've completed the relevant standard.

## Geography and territory

**Do you cover [my state / region]?**
NRPG operates across all Australian states and territories, plus New
Zealand. Coverage is denser in major metros; rural and remote
applicants are still very welcome.

**Will I have an exclusive territory?**
Territories are assigned based on stated service area plus existing
network coverage. We don't promise exclusivity — we promise that the
territory we assign matches your stated capability and current
network gaps. If you're the only contractor in a region for your
category, in practice that's effective exclusivity.

**Can I service multiple postcodes?**
Yes — the application asks for your service area. Territory
assignment may consolidate or split that based on network coverage.

## The application itself

**How long does the seven-step process take?**
End-to-end timing is subject to assessment — most applicants who
have their documents ready clear the document and identity steps
quickly, then competency tests + agreement signing follow at the
applicant's pace. We do not promise a specific timeframe.

**Can I save and come back later?**
Yes. The application is staged so you can save progress and return.

**What if I'm rejected?**
You'll be told why. The most common reasons are unverified IICRC
registration or insurance below the cover threshold. In most cases
the applicant can resolve the issue and reapply.

## After approval

**How are jobs assigned to me?**
Based on damage category, postcode / territory, your current
availability, and your performance KPIs from prior jobs. The
dispatch system flags matching jobs in the contractor portal.

**Do I have to accept every job offered?**
No. Decline rate is a KPI but a few declines for genuine reasons
(unavailable, outside scope, conflict of interest) won't affect
your standing.

**How do I get paid for the work?**
You bill the client directly. NRPG is not in the payment path
between you and the client. Your platform fee for each accepted job
is invoiced separately by NRPG and processed through your payment
method on file.

**What if a client doesn't pay?**
NRPG provides standard contract templates and dispute resolution
guidance, but ultimately the contractor-client commercial
relationship is the contractor's to manage.

## Compliance

**Is NRPG insurance-approved?**
NRPG is **IICRC-certified** through its network. We do not claim
"insurance-approved" status because that phrase implies a formal
insurer endorsement we do not hold. Most major Australian insurers
accept IICRC-certified work; specific acceptance varies by insurer
and policy.

**Do you do background checks?**
Yes — identity and professional verification at onboarding, plus
ongoing renewal of insurance and IICRC certification.

**What about contractor data privacy?**
Contractor data is handled under the Australian Privacy Principles
(APPs) and the NZ Privacy Act 2020 IPPs. See `disasterrecovery.com.au/privacy`
for the full notice.
