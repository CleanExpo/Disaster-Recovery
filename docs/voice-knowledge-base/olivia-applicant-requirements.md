# What an Applicant Needs to Bring

_Last updated: 2026-04-26._

This is the complete list of what a contractor applying to the NRPG
network needs ready BEFORE starting the application. The application
surface is staged so an applicant can begin without all of it, but
having these to hand makes onboarding much faster.

## Business identification

- **Australian Business Number (ABN)** — current, registered for GST
  if applicable.
- **NZ business identifier** — for New Zealand applicants, the
  equivalent NZBN works.
- **Trading name + legal entity name** — both, if different.
- **Business address** — primary trading address.
- **Principal contact** — name, role, mobile, email.

## Professional certification

- **IICRC registration** — for **each damage category** the applicant
  wants to be approved for. The IICRC register is public; NRPG
  verifies the registration number against it.
- Common IICRC certifications by category:
  - Water damage → **S500** (Water Damage Restoration)
  - Mould → **S520** (Mould Remediation)
  - Fire and smoke → **S700** (Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration)
  - Trauma and biohazard → **S540** (Trauma & Crime Scene)
  - Carpet / textile → **S100** / **S800**

## Insurance

- **Public liability insurance** — current certificate of currency
  (COI). The minimum cover required depends on the categories applied
  for; the application surface lists the figures.
- **Workers compensation insurance** — current COI, where required by
  the applicant's state or territory. (Sole traders without employees
  may have a different obligation; check with your state regulator.)
- **Professional indemnity insurance** — recommended but optional for
  most categories; required for categories that involve scope-of-works
  documentation work.

## Trade licences

Required where the relevant work is licensed in the applicant's state
or territory. Common examples:

- **Plumbing licence** — if the applicant performs water-pipe work as
  part of restoration (most don't, but some do).
- **Electrical licence** — same, for electrical safety make-safe work.
- **Asbestos removal licence** — required for asbestos categories
  (state-specific class A or class B licences).
- **Builders licence** — required if reconstruction work above a
  state-specific value threshold is part of the offering.

## Identity

- **Proof of identity** — for the **principal** of the business.
  Driver's licence + Medicare card OR passport. ID is verified
  electronically (no physical copies are stored).

## Service area

- The **postcodes or suburbs** the applicant currently services, or
  wants to service. Territory assignment is based on this stated area
  PLUS existing network coverage — the assigned territory may differ
  from the stated preference.

## Banking

For subscription payment processing only. Provided via Stripe
Checkout — the applicant enters card details directly into Stripe;
NRPG does not see or store card numbers.

## What you do NOT need

- A website (helpful but not required).
- Existing insurer panel approvals (NRPG matches based on IICRC
  certification, not insurer-specific endorsement).
- Historical job count or revenue threshold (no minimum trading
  history required, as long as IICRC + insurance + identity check
  out).
