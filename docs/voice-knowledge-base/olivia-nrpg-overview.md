# NRPG and Disaster Recovery — Overview

_Last updated: 2026-04-26._

## What NRPG is

**NRPG** stands for the **National Restoration Professionals Group Pty Ltd**.
It is an Australian company. NRPG operates a network of independent,
IICRC-certified restoration contractors across Australia and New Zealand.

## What Disaster Recovery is

**Disaster Recovery** is the consumer-facing brand that NRPG operates.
When a property owner has water, fire, storm, or other damage, they
contact Disaster Recovery. We then connect them with the right
certified contractor from the NRPG network for their location and
damage type.

## The model

NRPG is a **network orchestrator**. We do not perform restoration work
ourselves. The certified contractor performs the work.

- The **client** (property owner) is matched with a **contractor**.
- The **contractor** performs the restoration work and **bills the
  client directly** for that work.
- NRPG charges the contractor a **subscription** for network access
  plus a **per-job platform fee**. We do not take a share of the
  contractor's restoration revenue.
- NRPG does NOT bill the insurer. NRPG does NOT hold client funds in
  escrow.

This separation matters because it means:

1. The contractor sets their own pricing (within IICRC industry norms).
2. The contractor manages their own insurance liaison.
3. NRPG's role is matching, quality assurance, dispatch coordination,
   and standards enforcement — not commercial intermediary.

## Coverage

- **Australia** — all states and territories.
- **New Zealand** — coverage growing; NZ-specific consumer protections
  (CGA, FTA, NZ Privacy Act 2020 IPPs) apply.

## Standards

Every contractor in the network is **IICRC-certified**. IICRC stands
for the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification
— it is the global industry-standard body for restoration work.
Specific IICRC standards relevant to our categories include:

- **S500** — water damage restoration
- **S520** — mould remediation
- **S540** — trauma and crime scene cleanup
- **S700** — professional fire and smoke damage restoration
- **S800** — restoration of textile floor coverings

Certifications are verified against the public IICRC register at
application time and at renewal.
