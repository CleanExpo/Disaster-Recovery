## Purpose

This document defines the 15 target conversational queries to monitor for DR/NRPG appearing in Google Maps AI Overviews and "Ask Maps" responses. These are the queries most likely to surface local business results and AI-generated summaries for restoration services, particularly during and after severe weather events.

## 2. Monitoring Frequency

| Period | Frequency | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Active event window (TC Maila) | Weekly | While the event name is newsworthy and search volumes are elevated |
| Post-event wind-down (weeks 3–8 after landfall) | Fortnightly | As search volumes normalise |
| Standard ongoing monitoring | Monthly | Baseline tracking outside event windows |
| New named event (any cyclone/flood) | Weekly | Resume weekly monitoring for the duration of the event window |

The TC Maila active window begins at confirmation of Queensland landfall. Resume weekly checks immediately upon any Category 2+ system threatening the QLD coast.

---

## 3. How to Check

### Method — Google Maps (desktop or mobile)

1. Open Google Maps (maps.google.com.au or the Maps app)
2. Enter each query from the list above into the search bar exactly as written
3. Note the following for each query:
   - Does an AI Overview / "Ask Maps" summary panel appear at the top of results?
   - If yes: Is disasterrecovery.com.au or NRPG cited or linked within the summary?
   - What are the top 3 local business results shown in the map pack?
   - Is NRPG / Disaster Recovery listed in the top 3?
4. Screenshot any result where DR/NRPG appears in the AI Overview or map pack top 3
5. Log the result in the tracking table below

### Notes on checking

- Use an incognito / private browser window to avoid personalised results
- Check from an Australian IP address (results differ by region)
- For geo-specific queries (e.g., Cairns, FNQ), switch Google Maps location to that region before searching — use the "Search this area" function if results do not auto-localise
- Check both desktop and mobile for event-specific queries, as AI Overview display differs by device

## 5. Success Criteria

**Primary goal:**
Appear in AI Overview or top 3 local results for **8 or more of the 15 queries** within **60 days of TC Maila event** (target date: approximately 2026-06-09, adjusted to actual landfall date).

**Secondary goals:**
- Appear in AI Overview for at least 3 of the 5 Category A (emergency/high intent) queries
- Appear in AI Overview or map pack for at least 2 of the 4 Category B (TC Maila event-specific) queries within 14 days of landfall
- Appear in at least 2 of the 3 Category C (insurance/IICRC) queries within 60 days

**Stretch goal:**
Named in AI Overview summary text (not just as a linked result) for at least 2 queries.

## 6. Supporting Actions
