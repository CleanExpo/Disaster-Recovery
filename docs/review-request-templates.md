## How to Send

2. Choose email or SMS based on client preference

5. Do NOT offer incentives for reviews (Google policy violation)

## What Clients Should Mention (Ask Maps Signal Value)

Ask clients to naturally include these details in their review — each one expands our Ask Maps query surface:

- **Service type**: "water damage restoration", "flood damage", "fire damage", "mould remediation", "storm damage"
- **Location**: suburb, city, or region (e.g. "Brisbane", "Gold Coast", "Far North Queensland")
- **Speed**: "responded within the hour", "same-day response", "24/7 availability"
- **Insurance**: "helped with our insurance claim", "worked with our insurer", "provided documentation"
- **Certification**: "IICRC certified", "certified contractors", "professional team"

**Example prompt to clients:**
> "When you write your review, it helps other people find us if you mention what type of damage you had, where you're located, and how the process went with your insurance claim — but only include what's true for you."
