NRP-002: Environmental Assessment & Testing

Professional Testing Protocols & Australian Standards Compliance

1. Environmental Testing Overview

Testing Requirements

Environmental assessment and testing is mandatory under Australian regulations for:

  • Asbestos identification and assessment (AS 4964:2004)
  • Lead paint testing (AS/NZS 4361.2:2017)
  • Mould sampling and analysis (AS/NZS 3666:2011)
  • Indoor air quality assessment (AS 1668.2:2012)
  • Soil contamination testing (NEPM 2013)
Legal Requirement: All environmental testing must be conducted by NATA accredited laboratories. Testing results are valid for insurance claims and legal proceedings only when performed to Australian Standards.

Equipment: TSI AirAssure, particle counters, VOC meters

Applications: Continuous monitoring, compliance verification

4. Sampling Protocols & Procedures

Asbestos Sampling Protocol

Pre-sampling Requirements:

  1. Visual inspection and documentation
  2. SafeWork notification (if required)
  3. PPE: P2 mask, disposable overalls, gloves
  4. Wet sampling to minimize fiber release
  5. Chain of custody documentation
Australian Requirement: All asbestos samples must be analyzed by NATA accredited laboratories using Polarized Light Microscopy (PLM) as per AS 4964:2004.
