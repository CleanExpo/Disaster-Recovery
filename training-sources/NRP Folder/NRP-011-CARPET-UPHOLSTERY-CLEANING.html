⚠ Fibre Testing Methods

  • Burn Test: Different fibres burn distinctively (perform in safe area)
  • Water Test: Wool absorbs slowly, synthetics repel
  • Bleach Test: 10% bleach dissolves wool/silk in 3 minutes
  • Touch Test: Wool feels warm, synthetics feel cool

Cleaning Methods Comparison

💧

Hot Water Extraction (HWE)

Dry Time: 4-8 hours | Cost: $0.35-0.55/m² | Effectiveness: 95%

IICRC preferred method. Deep cleaning with truckmount or portable units. 80-100°C water, 100-500 PSI.

🔄

Encapsulation

Dry Time: 30-60 minutes | Cost: $0.15-0.25/m² | Effectiveness: 75%

Low moisture, crystallising polymers. Ideal for commercial maintenance. 15-20L per 100m².

🧹

Dry Compound

Dry Time: Immediate | Cost: $0.25-0.35/m² | Effectiveness: 65%

Absorbent compound with solvents. No drying time. Good for water-sensitive carpets.

🌀

Bonnet Cleaning

Dry Time: 1-2 hours | Cost: $0.10-0.20/m² | Effectiveness: 60%

Surface cleaning with rotary machine. Quick interim maintenance for commercial carpets.

Quality Standards & Inspection

AS/NZS 3733:2018 Inspection Criteria

  • Visual Inspection: No visible soil, spots, or residue from 2 metres standing
  • Texture: Carpet pile standing upright, no matting or distortion
  • Odour: No offensive odours, fresh clean scent
  • Moisture: Less than 15% moisture content within 4 hours
  • pH Level: Between 6.0 and 8.0 after cleaning
  • Re-soiling: No rapid re-soiling within 30 days

Pre-Inspection Checklist

  • Fibre identification test
  • Colourfast testing
  • Pre-existing damage documentation
