1. Australian Work Health & Safety Overview

Legal Requirement: Under the Work Health and Safety Act 2011, all businesses have a legal duty of care to ensure workplace safety. Penalties for breaches can reach $3.3 million for corporations and $660,000 for individuals, plus potential jail time for serious breaches.
WHS Requirement Legal Framework Maximum Penalty (Corp) Maximum Penalty (Individual) Enforcement Agency
Duty of Care (Primary) WHS Act 2011 - Section 19 $3,300,000 $660,000 + 5 years jail SafeWork Australia
PPE Provision WHS Regulation 2011 - Part 3.1 $660,000 $132,000 State/Territory WHS
Hazardous Substances WHS Regulation 2011 - Chapter 7 $1,650,000 $330,000 + 2 years jail State/Territory WHS
Training & Supervision WHS Act 2011 - Section 19(3) $660,000 $132,000 State/Territory WHS
Incident Reporting WHS Act 2011 - Section 38 $132,000 $26,400 State/Territory WHS
2024 Penalty Rates: Category 1: $3.3M/$660K | Category 2: $1.65M/$330K | Category 3: $660K/$132K | Category 4: $132K/$26.4K

3. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

Work Activity Required PPE Australian Standard Replacement Schedule Average Cost (AUD)

Asbestos Removal (Class B) Full-face P3 respirator, disposable coveralls, gloves AS/NZS 1716:2012, AS/NZS 4123:2014 Single use (except respirator) $120-200 per set
Chemical Handling Chemical resistant gloves, apron, safety glasses AS/NZS 2161.10.1-10.15:2005 Per chemical compatibility $35-65 per set
Electrical Work Insulated gloves, arc flash suit, safety helmet AS/NZS 2381:2000, AS/NZS 1801:1997 Gloves: 6 months, Suit: annual test $180-350 per set
General Construction Hard hat, safety boots, high-vis vest, safety glasses AS/NZS 1801:1997, AS/NZS 4602.1:2011 Hard hat: 5 years, Boots: 12 months $75-120 per set
PPE Selection Hierarchy: AS/NZS 4801:2001 requires selection based on hazard assessment. Always choose PPE with current Australian Standards certification and ensure proper fit testing for respiratory protection.
