Communication Templates & Scripts

Scenario 1: Emergency Water Damage Call

"I understand how stressful this situation must be. Let me help you right away. First, for your safety, can you turn off the water main if it's still running? I'm dispatching our emergency team now - they'll be there within [timeframe]. In the meantime, if it's safe, try to move any valuable items to dry areas. We'll take care of everything else when we arrive. Is everyone in your home safe and is there anything urgent I should know?"

Scenario 2: Angry Customer - Delay in Service

"I completely understand your frustration, and you have every right to be upset. Let me personally look into this immediately. I can see we were scheduled for [time] and that hasn't happened - that's not acceptable. Let me get our team redirected to your property right now. I'll also apply a service credit for this inconvenience. Can I have them there within the next hour? I'll personally monitor this and call you back in 15 minutes with confirmation."

Scenario 3: Insurance Claim Confusion

"I know insurance claims can be overwhelming. Let me simplify this for you. We work directly with [insurer] all the time. Here's what happens: We'll document everything today, send our report directly to your adjuster, and we can usually start emergency work immediately under your policy. You won't need to pay anything upfront for covered work. I'll handle all the paperwork. All you need to do is sign our authorisation form. Does that help clarify things?"

Complaint Resolution Framework

L.E.A.R.N. Method

  • Listen without interrupting
  • Empathise with their situation
  • Apologise sincerely
  • Resolve the issue
  • Notify of prevention measures

Resolution Authority Levels

  • Technician: Up to $500 credit
  • Supervisor: Up to $2,000
  • Manager: Up to $5,000
  • Director: Unlimited
  • Escalation: Within 2 hours

Service Recovery Tactics

  • Immediate response (< 1 hour)
  • Service credits/discounts
  • Priority scheduling
  • Additional services free
  • Personal follow-up
