NRP-009: Contents Restoration & Pack-Out

Professional Inventory Management & Restoration Protocols

1. Contents Processing Categories

Category A - Non-Restorable

Definition: Items with no restoration value or health hazard

Examples: Particle board furniture, saturated mattresses, contaminated cosmetics

Action: Document, photograph, dispose per regulations

Insurance: Total loss claim documentation required

Category B - Restorable On-Site

Definition: Items that can be cleaned in place

Examples: Hard furniture, appliances, window treatments

Action: Clean, sanitise, deodorise in position

Timeline: Concurrent with structural restoration

Category C - Pack-Out Required

Definition: Items requiring off-site restoration

Examples: Electronics, artwork, textiles, documents

Action: Inventory, pack, transport to facility

Processing: Specialised cleaning and restoration

Category D - Storage Only

Definition: Unaffected items requiring temporary storage

Examples: Furniture from unaffected rooms

Action: Inventory, protect, store, return

Duration: Until structure restoration complete

Australian Consumer Law: All contents restoration must meet "fit for purpose" standards. Items must be restored to pre-loss condition or replaced with equivalent quality. Documentation of all processes is mandatory for warranty compliance.

3. Restoration Technologies & Equipment

Esporta Wash System

Technology: Hydraulic pressure washing

Capability: Soft contents to food grade clean

Items: Clothing, leather, sports equipment

Contamination: Category 3 water, heavy smoke

Cost: $45-85/kg processed

Availability: Major cities only

Ultrasonic Cleaning

Technology: High-frequency sound waves

Capability: Removes microscopic contamination

Items: Electronics, jewellery, blinds

Tank Size: Up to 2m x 1m items

Cost: $120-250/hour operation

Processing: 30 min - 2 hours per batch

Freeze Drying System

Technology: Sublimation drying

Capability: Document and book restoration

Capacity: 500kg per chamber

Success Rate: 85% for water damage

Cost: $35-60/kg documents

Timeline: 5-14 days complete

Ozone Chamber

Technology: O3 oxidation treatment

Capability: Odour elimination

Items: All non-porous materials

Chamber Size: 20m³ walk-in

Cost: $150-300/chamber load

Duration: 24-48 hours treatment

Gamma Irradiation

Technology: Sterilisation via radiation

Capability: Eliminates all biological contamination

Items: Documents, artwork, textiles

Location: ANSTO facility, Lucas Heights

Cost: $450-850/pallet

Certification: TGA approved process

Thermal Desorption

Technology: Controlled heat treatment

Capability: VOC and odour removal

Items: Building materials, furniture

Temperature: 65-80°C controlled

Cost: $85-150/m³

Duration: 8-24 hours
