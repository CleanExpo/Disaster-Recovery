2. Claims Processing Workflow

Standard Australian Insurance Claims Process

1 Initial Assessment & Notification

Customer reports claim through insurer portal or phone. Claim number assigned within 24 hours. Initial assessment determines complexity and priority level.

2 Loss Adjuster Assignment

Independent loss adjuster appointed for claims over $10,000. Site inspection scheduled within 48-72 hours. Preliminary damage assessment conducted.

3 Contractor Selection & Scope

Preferred contractor network accessed. Multiple quotes obtained for transparency. Detailed scope of works prepared using Xactimate or similar.

4 Authorization & Work Commencement

Formal work authorization issued. Certificate of currency verified. Work commences with regular progress reporting requirements.

5 Quality Control & Completion

Progress inspections conducted. Quality standards verified against scope. Final inspection and customer satisfaction confirmed.

6 Invoice Processing & Payment

Tax invoice submitted with supporting documentation. Payment processed according to terms (typically NET 14-45 days depending on insurer).

Processing Times by Claim Value (2024 Australian Average):
• Under $5,000: 3-7 business days
• $5,000-$25,000: 7-14 business days
• $25,000-$100,000: 14-28 business days
• Over $100,000: 28-60 business days

Critical Requirement: All contractors must maintain current Professional Indemnity insurance minimum $5M and Public Liability minimum $20M as per Australian insurance industry standards. Failure to maintain coverage voids all agreements.
