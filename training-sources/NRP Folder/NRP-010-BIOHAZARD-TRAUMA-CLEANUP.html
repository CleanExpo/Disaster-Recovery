Module Overview

This module covers specialised biohazard and trauma cleanup procedures following Australian workplace health and safety standards. You will learn proper protocols, safety equipment requirements, and decontamination procedures for handling biological hazards and trauma scenes.

Biohazard Cleanup Procedures

1. Scene Assessment

  • Police clearance obtained
  • Hazard identification
  • Photography documentation
  • Establish containment zones
  • Air quality testing

2. Containment Setup

  • Red zone establishment
  • Negative air pressure
  • Decontamination chamber
  • Waste staging area
  • Warning signage

3. Removal Process

  • Gross contamination removal
  • Surface cleaning
  • Disinfection application
  • ATP testing verification
  • Odour neutralisation

4. Waste Disposal

  • UN3291 certified containers
  • Manifest documentation
  • Licensed transporter
  • Incineration facility
  • Chain of custody

Psychological Support & Team Welfare

Mental Health Awareness

Trauma cleanup can have significant psychological impacts. Australian companies must provide:

  • Employee Assistance Programs (EAP)
  • Critical incident debriefing within 24 hours
  • Access to counselling services
  • Rotation of duties to prevent burnout
  • Regular mental health check-ins

Support Services: Beyond Blue: 1300 22 4636 | Lifeline: 13 11 14
