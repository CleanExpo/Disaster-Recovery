Preventive Maintenance Schedule

Daily Tasks (5 minutes per unit)

  • Visual inspection for damage
  • Check power cords and plugs
  • Clean air intakes and filters
  • Record hour meter readings
DAILY

Weekly Tasks (15 minutes per unit)

  • Deep clean all filters
  • Check and tighten connections
  • Test safety switches
  • Lubricate moving parts
WEEKLY

Monthly Tasks (30 minutes per unit)

  • Calibrate moisture meters
  • Check refrigerant levels
  • Clean coils and heat exchangers
  • Update service logs
MONTHLY

Annual Tasks (2-4 hours per unit)

  • Complete overhaul and service
  • Replace wear items
  • Electrical safety testing (AS/NZS 3760)
  • Update asset register
ANNUAL

Australian Compliance Requirements

⚠ Electrical Safety Standards

  • AS/NZS 3760:2022 - In-service safety inspection and testing
  • Testing Frequency: 3 months for construction sites, 6 months for industrial
  • Test & Tag Colours 2024: Orange (Jan-Jun), Blue (Jul-Dec)
  • RCD Protection: Required for all portable equipment
  • Documentation: Test records retained for 7 years
  • Penalties: Up to $50,000 for non-compliance
