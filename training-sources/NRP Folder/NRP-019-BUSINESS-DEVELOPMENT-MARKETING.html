Digital Marketing Best Practices

🎯 Google Ads Strategy for Restoration

  • Keywords: "water damage [suburb]", "emergency plumber leak", "flood restoration near me"
  • Budget: $100-150/day minimum for metro areas
  • Ad Extensions: Call, location, sitelinks, callouts
  • Landing Pages: Service-specific with forms
  • Quality Score: Aim for 7+ (reduces cost by 30%)
  • Conversion Tracking: Calls, forms, chat
  • Negative Keywords: DIY, hire, equipment, jobs

SEO Essentials

  • Google My Business optimisation
  • Local citations (50+ directories)
  • Service area pages
  • Before/after galleries
  • Customer reviews (4.5+ stars)
  • Mobile responsive design
  • Page speed < 3 seconds
