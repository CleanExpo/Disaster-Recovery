MODULE-004: Fire Damage Recovery Process

Duration: 15 minutes | Category: Fire Damage

Introduction

Fire and smoke damage restoration involves complex processes including structural assessment, odor removal, and content restoration. Understanding the timeline helps set realistic expectations.

Assessment and Safety Inspection

  • Structural engineer assessment (if required)
  • Electrical safety inspection
  • Air quality testing
  • Hazardous material identification (asbestos, lead)

Restoration Phases

  1. Emergency Board-Up & Tarping (Day 1)
  2. Smoke Odor Removal (Days 2-7)
  3. Soot Cleaning (Days 3-10)
  4. Content Restoration (Weeks 1-4)
  5. Structural Repairs (Weeks 2-8)
  6. Final Cleaning & Deodorization (Week 8+)

Typical Timeline for Fire Recovery:

  • Minor fire (single room): 2-4 weeks
  • Moderate fire (multiple rooms): 4-12 weeks
  • Severe fire (whole property): 3-6 months

Living Arrangements During Restoration

Most fire damage requires temporary relocation. Your insurance typically covers alternative accommodation. NRPG can help coordinate with your insurer.

Do Not Return Until Cleared: Fire-damaged properties may have unsafe air quality, structural issues, or electrical hazards. Wait for professional clearance.

Key Takeaways

  • Fire restoration is complex and time-consuming
  • Professional assessment is critical for safety
  • Smoke odor requires specialized treatment
  • Many items can be restored (not always replacement)
  • Expect 2 weeks to 6 months depending on severity
