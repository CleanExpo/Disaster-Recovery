MODULE-007: Emergency Preparedness & Prevention

Duration: 15 minutes | Category: Prevention

Emergency Kit Essentials

Australian Emergency Kit Checklist:

  • Water (3 liters per person per day for 3 days)
  • Non-perishable food (3-day supply)
  • Battery-powered or hand-crank radio
  • Torch and extra batteries
  • First aid kit with Australian standards compliance
  • Medications (7-day supply minimum)
  • Important documents in waterproof container
  • Cash ($500+ in small notes)
  • Phone charger and power bank
  • Emergency contact list (printed copy)
  • Whistle (to signal for help)
  • Dust masks (for smoke or debris)
  • Plastic sheeting and duct tape (emergency sealing)

Important Documents to Safeguard

  • Insurance: Home, contents, car, health policies
  • Property: Deeds, titles, mortgage documents
  • Identification: Passports, birth certificates, driver licenses
  • Financial: Bank details, investment records
  • Medical: Prescriptions, medical history
  • Photos: Pictures of valuable items (for claims)

Risk Mitigation by Disaster Type

Water Damage Prevention:

  • Know main water valve location (practice turning off)
  • Replace washing machine hoses every 5 years
  • Install leak detectors near appliances
  • Clear gutters twice yearly
  • Grade soil away from foundation

Fire Prevention:

  • Test smoke alarms monthly (replace batteries annually)
  • Service electrical appliances regularly
  • Don't overload power boards
  • Keep flammable materials away from heat sources
  • Have fire extinguisher accessible (check annually)

Storm Preparation:

  • Trim overhanging tree branches
  • Secure outdoor furniture before storms
  • Clear roof gutters and downpipes
  • Check roof tiles/sheets for damage
  • Keep emergency tarps and sandbags ready (flood-prone areas)

Evacuation Planning

  1. Identify evacuation routes from your property
  2. Know where your local evacuation center is
  3. Plan for pets (identify pet-friendly accommodation)
  4. Designate meeting point for family members
  5. Keep car fueled (at least half tank in storm season)
