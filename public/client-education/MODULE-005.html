MODULE-005: Mold Remediation Explained

Duration: 12 minutes | Category: Mold

Introduction

Mold growth is common in Australia's humid climate. Understanding when to call professionals and what to expect from remediation prevents health risks and property damage.

Health Risks of Mold Exposure

Health Symptoms May Include:

  • Respiratory issues (coughing, wheezing, shortness of breath)
  • Allergic reactions (sneezing, runny nose, red eyes)
  • Skin irritation
  • Headaches and fatigue
  • Asthma attacks (in sensitive individuals)

Seek medical attention if experiencing symptoms. Some molds produce mycotoxins that can be serious health hazards.

Common Causes of Mold Growth

  • Water leaks (pipes, roofs, windows)
  • Flooding or water damage
  • High humidity (coastal areas, bathrooms)
  • Poor ventilation
  • Condensation
  • Damp basements or crawl spaces

When to Call Professionals

Call IICRC-Certified Mold Remediation Immediately If:

  • Mold covers more than 1 square meter
  • Mold is in HVAC system
  • You have health issues
  • Mold returns after DIY cleaning
  • Strong musty odor throughout property
  • Mold from sewage or contaminated water

Professional Remediation Process

  1. Assessment & Testing: Identify mold type, extent, moisture source
  2. Containment: Seal affected areas to prevent spread
  3. Air Filtration: HEPA filters capture airborne spores
  4. Removal: Safe removal of contaminated materials
  5. Cleaning: Antimicrobial treatment of surfaces
  6. Drying: Eliminate moisture source, dry thoroughly
  7. Restoration: Repair or replace damaged materials
  8. Verification: Post-remediation testing

Prevention Strategies

  • Fix leaks immediately
  • Maintain proper ventilation (exhaust fans in bathrooms)
  • Use dehumidifiers in humid areas
  • Clean gutters regularly
  • Inspect roof annually
  • Monitor indoor humidity (keep below 60%)

Key Takeaways

  • Mold is a health hazard - take seriously
  • Professional assessment is necessary (don't guess)
  • Remediation requires containment and proper equipment
  • Address moisture source or mold will return
  • Prevention is cheaper than remediation
