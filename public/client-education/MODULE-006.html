MODULE-006: Your Rights as a Property Owner

Duration: 10 minutes | Category: Legal Rights

Australian Consumer Law Protections

The Australian Consumer Law (ACL) provides protections when you hire disaster recovery contractors.

Your Rights Under ACL:

  • Services must be provided with due care and skill
  • Services must be fit for purpose
  • Services must be completed within reasonable time
  • If work is defective, contractor must fix at no extra cost
  • You may be entitled to compensation for defects

Warranty Periods

  • Workmanship: 12 months minimum (NRPG: 24 months)
  • Materials: As per manufacturer (typically 1-10 years)
  • Structural work: Check state building regulations
NRPG Guarantee: All NRPG work comes with a 24-month workmanship warranty and satisfaction guarantee.

Dispute Resolution Options

  1. Direct negotiation with contractor
  2. Contact Fair Trading in your state
  3. AFCA (Australian Financial Complaints Authority) for insurance disputes
  4. State tribunal (NCAT, VCAT, QCAT, etc.)
  5. Legal action (last resort)

Key Takeaways

  • Australian Consumer Law protects you
  • Warranties are mandatory (minimum 12 months)
  • Multiple dispute resolution options available
  • Fair Trading provides free assistance
  • NRPG offers extended warranties beyond legal minimums
