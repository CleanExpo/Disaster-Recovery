MODULE-002: Understanding Your Insurance Claim

Duration: 12 minutes | Category: Insurance Claims

Introduction

Navigating insurance claims can be complex. This module focuses on the NRPG process and how we work with your insurer, without providing insurer-specific advice (always verify details with your insurance provider).

NRPG Insurance Support

NRPG contractors are experienced in working with major Australian insurers and can provide documentation that meets insurance requirements. We assist with claim preparation but always defer to your insurer's specific policy terms.

Types of Coverage (General)

Building Insurance vs Contents Insurance

  • Building Insurance: Covers structure, fixed items (kitchen, bathroom, built-in wardrobes)
  • Contents Insurance: Covers movable items (furniture, electronics, clothing)
  • Combined Policies: Many Australians have both in one policy
Check Your Policy: Coverage varies significantly between insurers. Some policies have specific exclusions for flood, earthquake, or gradual damage. Review your Product Disclosure Statement (PDS).

The Claims Process with NRPG

Typical Timeline (with NRPG Support):

  1. Day 0-1: Damage occurs, you contact NRPG and your insurer
  2. Day 1-2: NRPG contractor inspects, provides emergency mitigation
  3. Day 2-7: Insurance assessor visits, NRPG provides documentation
  4. Day 7-14: Insurer approves scope of work
  5. Day 14-30: NRPG completes restoration work
  6. Day 30-45: Final inspection, claim settlement

Timelines vary by claim complexity and insurer responsiveness.

Required Documentation

What NRPG Contractors Provide:

  • Detailed scope of work
  • Itemized cost estimates
  • Before, during, and after photos
  • Moisture readings and drying logs
  • Material specifications
  • Compliance certifications (IICRC standards)
  • Warranty documentation

What You Need to Provide:

  • Photos/videos of damage (immediately after occurrence)
  • Policy number and contact details
  • List of damaged items with approximate values
  • Receipts for valuable items (if available)
  • Previous inspection or maintenance records

Understanding Excess (Deductibles)

Excess is the amount you pay before insurance coverage applies.

Common Excess Structures in Australia:

  • Basic Excess: $500 - $2,000 (standard policies)
  • Age Excess: Additional excess for policyholders over 65-70
  • Voluntary Excess: Higher excess = lower premiums
  • Flood Excess: Often higher ($1,000 - $10,000)
NRPG Process: You pay your excess to NRPG upon job commencement. NRPG then invoices your insurer directly for the remaining approved amount. This simplifies the process - you don't pay the full amount upfront.

Common Reasons for Claim Denial

❌ Claims Often Rejected For:

  • Delayed Reporting: Not notifying insurer within required timeframe (usually 24-48 hours)
  • Lack of Maintenance: Damage from wear-and-tear or poor maintenance
  • Excluded Events: Damage from floods (if no flood cover), earthquakes, etc.
  • Unlicensed Repairs: Using non-certified contractors voids some policies
  • Insufficient Documentation: No photos, no proof of damage extent
  • Pre-existing Damage: Damage that existed before policy start date

Disputes and Appeals

If Your Claim is Denied or Underpaid:

  1. Request Written Explanation: Insurers must provide reasons in writing
  2. Review Your PDS: Check if denial is valid per policy terms
  3. Internal Dispute Resolution: Request escalation within insurance company
  4. Financial Ombudsman Service (AFCA): Free external dispute resolution for Australians
  5. Legal Advice: Consult insurance lawyer if significant claim

NRPG Support: We can provide additional documentation, expert reports, and evidence to support your appeal. Our detailed restoration records often help overturn initial denials.

Payment Process Through NRPG

How Payment Works:

  1. Job Approval: Insurer approves NRPG's scope and cost estimate
  2. Excess Payment: You pay your excess amount to NRPG (pre-authorization hold)
  3. Work Commencement: NRPG begins restoration immediately
  4. Progress Claims: For large jobs, NRPG may claim in stages
  5. Completion: Final inspection, you approve work
  6. Settlement: NRPG invoices insurer for approved amount minus excess
  7. Your Payment: Excess charge processed (you've already pre-authorized)

Tips for Faster Claim Approval

  • Report damage to insurer within 24 hours
  • Take extensive photos before any cleanup
  • Keep all receipts (accommodation, meals, emergency expenses)
  • Respond promptly to insurer requests for information
  • Use NRPG-certified contractors (insurers trust certification)
  • Don't throw away damaged items until assessor has seen them
  • Maintain clear communication records (dates, names, reference numbers)
  • Be honest and accurate in your claim details

Emergency Accommodation & Additional Expenses

Many Australian insurance policies cover:

  • Temporary accommodation (hotel, rental) if home is uninhabitable
  • Storage fees for furniture and belongings
  • Meals (if kitchen is unusable)
  • Pet boarding (some policies)
Keep All Receipts: These additional living expenses (ALE) are usually reimbursable, but you must provide receipts. Check your policy limits (often capped at 10-20% of building sum insured).

Key Takeaways

  1. Report within 24 hours - Policy requirement
  2. Document extensively - Photos are your best evidence
  3. NRPG handles insurer liaison - We provide compliant documentation
  4. You only pay your excess - NRPG invoices insurer directly
  5. IICRC certification matters - Insurers trust certified contractors
  6. Keep communication records - Dates, names, reference numbers
  7. Know your rights - AFCA provides free dispute resolution
