# Disaster Recovery Domain Knowledge

**Skill ID**: disaster-recovery-domain
**Version**: 1.0.0

## Service Types (Australian)

### Water Damage
- Category 1: Clean water (supply lines)
- Category 2: Grey water (washing machines, dishwashers)
- Category 3: Black water (sewage, flooding)

### Fire & Smoke Damage
- Primary fire damage
- Secondary smoke damage
- Structural damage assessment

### Mould Remediation
- Visible mould growth
- Hidden mould (cavities, HVAC)
- Air quality testing
- Containment procedures

### Biohazard Cleanup
- Crime scene cleanup
- Deceased estate cleanup
- Infectious disease cleanup
- Hoarding cleanup

## IICRC Standards

### S500 - Water Damage Restoration
- Inspection and assessment
- Water classification
- Structural drying
- Monitoring

### S520 - Mould Remediation
- Source identification
- Containment procedures
- Removal and cleaning
- Post-remediation verification

### S700 - Fire & Smoke Damage
- Inspection and testing
- Cleaning methods
- Deodorization
- Restoration

### S001 - Professional Standards
- Ethics and conduct
- Professional practices
- Customer service standards

## Australian Regulations

### States
- NSW: Building Code compliance

### Insurance Integration
- NRMA, Suncorp, Allianz, QBE
- IAG, CGU, Medibank
- Claim lodgment procedures

## Emergency Response Levels
- URGENT: <2 hours (active water, biohazard)
- HIGH: <4 hours (fire, mould)
- STANDARD: <24 hours (assessment)
- SCHEDULED: Pre-booked inspections
